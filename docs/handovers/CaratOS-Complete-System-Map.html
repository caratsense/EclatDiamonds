CaratOS system map

One platform that turns customer activity into organised work.

A plain-language guide for someone seeing CaratOS for the first time. CaratOS is a multi-company CRM and operations platform; Eclat is its jewellery implementation, not the whole product.

1. People arrive

Ads, WhatsApp, phone, walk-in, QR, website or imported data
→

2. CaratOS recognises them

Matches phone/email to one tenant’s customer profile
→

3. Work is created

Lead, conversation, follow-up, service task or operational record
→

4. The right team handles it

Branch routing, assignment, consent, AI draft and human approval
→

5. Results are measured

Sale, response, attendance, campaign attribution and return on spend
CUSTOMER

Who is the person?

A permanent profile containing identity, contact details, interactions and history.

LEAD

What opportunity exists?

A sales enquiry with source, location, owner, value, stage and outcome.

TENANT

Whose data is it?

Each client organisation has isolated users, branches, customers, rules and integrations.

The essential rule: a customer and a lead are not the same. Importing a customer does not create a fake opportunity. A lead appears only when a real enquiry or sales opportunity occurs.

Is this a real sales opportunity?

No

Keep the customer/conversation/activity, but do not manufacture a lead. Example: ordinary support WhatsApp or a customer-only import.

Yes

Determine a location, create or reuse the opportunity, record its source, and assign it safely.

Glossary

Webhook
A provider calling CaratOS when something happens.
Adapter
A translator between CaratOS and another system.
Idempotent
Repeating the same event does not create duplicates.
Attribution
Evidence of which campaign produced an opportunity/result.
Tenant
One client organisation and its isolated data.
Outbox
The durable queue of messages waiting for delivery.
