Part One

Getting started

Signing in, what each role sees, and how to give somebody the access they need — including the two things that most often go wrong.

Signing in

Opening the address goes straight to sign-in. There is no public landing page to click past.

MethodWho uses it
Login ID + password Head office, managers, and anyone an admin has set a password for.
WhatsApp OTP Shop-floor staff. They enter their mobile number and receive a code, valid for 5 minutes. Nothing to forget or share.

Login IDs follow a pattern

Accounts are created as firstname.storename@eclatdiamonds.in — for example anjali.mumbaibandra@eclatdiamonds.in. Nothing tells staff this automatically, so an admin must pass it on.

The most common sign-in failure

“Email ID not found” almost always means somebody is guessing their login. Read the exact ID from Settings → Team. An account made outside the pattern keeps whatever address it was created with, and a login ID cannot be changed afterwards.

Where each role lands

RoleSeesLands on
Head officeEvery branch, every screen, all reporting.Dashboards
Area managerThe branches in their area.Punch screen
Store managerTheir branch: inbox, leads, team, attendance, stock.Punch screen
SalespersonTheir branch's customers, and their own attendance.Punch screen

Everyone except head office lands on the attendance punch screen, which passes them through to their own home once the day is started.

Giving people access

Add a person — Settings → Team

  1. Open Settings → Team and choose Add staff.
  2. Enter name, mobile number and branch, and pick a role.
  3. Optionally set a password. Leave it blank and they sign in by WhatsApp OTP.
  4. The screen shows their Login ID. Write it down and give it to them — nothing sends it automatically.

Set or reset a password — Settings → Stores

Not on the Team screen

Password reset lives under Settings → Stores: open the branch, find the person, choose Reset password. The Team screen says “reset on their row”, which is misleading — the control is on Stores.

Choose which screens — Settings → People & Access

Head office can grant or withdraw individual screens per person. Left alone, each role gets its standard set, which suits most people.

One setting that silently breaks lead routing

A store manager who cannot open Conversations is skipped when the system looks for an owner for a new lead. The lead reaches the branch and sits unowned. If leads stop being assigned, check this first.

Grant daily-report access — Settings → WhatsApp Reporting

  1. Open Settings → WhatsApp Reporting. Everyone in your branches is listed with who can already file.
  2. Choose Set up on their row. A code appears and is copied to your clipboard. It lasts 10 minutes.
  3. Read the code to them. They send it from the handset they want to use, to the staff reporting number.
  4. That handset is now theirs for reporting. Remove undoes it.
Why you never type their phone number

The number is learnt from the message carrying the code, so nobody can bind a handset they are not holding. The bot also cannot message a number that has never written to it — WhatsApp only lets a business open a conversation with an approved template, and a one-time code is not something Meta approves.

Part Three

Customers and leads

How an enquiry arrives, who it reaches, how it is scored, and the two WhatsApp numbers that carry it.

Two numbers, two jobs. A message is answered by whichever number it arrives on.

What the customer bot asks

  1. What are you looking for (engagement ring · wedding jewellery · daily wear · other)
  2. Approximate budget (under ₹50K · ₹50K–₹1L · ₹1L–₹2L · above ₹2L)
  3. When you need it (within 7 days · this month · in a few months · just exploring)
  4. Shall we call (yes now · later · not now)

Customers may answer in their own words — the options are a prompt, not a menu. After the last answer the bot stops and the branch is notified.

One phone, both bots, no collision

The same mobile can hold a customer conversation on one line and a staff reporting conversation on the other at once. Neither overwrites the other, so staff can safely test the customer bot from their own phone.

The bot stops once a person takes over

After hand-over the thread is marked for a human and the bot stays silent in it. When testing, always use a number that has never written in, or it will look as though nothing is happening.

Conversations

What happens when a lead arrives

  1. The customer taps an advert or writes to the customer number.
  2. If they came from an advert, a routing rule matches it to a showroom and stamps the conversation with that branch.
  3. The bot asks its four questions.
  4. The conversation is handed to that branch's store manager, who is notified by name.
  5. The thread shows the advert tapped, the answers given, and a lead score.

Moving a lead to someone else

Open the conversation and use Reassign. Choose the branch first, then the owner — the list only offers people who work at that branch. The new owner is notified; the person making the change is not.

The 24-hour rule

Why the message box sometimes will not type

WhatsApp allows a free reply only within 24 hours of the customer's last message. After that the box is disabled and says so; an approved template can still reach them. This is WhatsApp's rule, not a setting, and the clock restarts whenever the customer writes again.

Reading the inbox

Advert routing

A rule sends each campaign's leads to the showroom that paid for it, so the right manager is notified within seconds.

Setting a rule — Settings → Business Configuration → Ad routing

Match onUse when
Ad-set name contains The recommended choice. One rule per showroom, matching words your marketing team already puts in the ad-set name — “Bandra”, “Udaipur”. Every future advert in that ad set routes with no further work.
Exact ad ID Pinning one advert somewhere different, or a campaign whose ad-set name carries no location.

Where two rules match, the more specific wins — “Bandra Broadway” beats “Bandra” with no manual ordering.

Ad-set name matching needs the Meta Ads connection

A Click-to-WhatsApp tap reports the advert's ID and nothing else. To turn that into an ad-set name, connect Meta Ads under Settings → Integrations with a token that can read adverts. Without it, only exact-ad-ID rules can match.

If a lead arrives unrouted

  1. Open the thread and read the advert card — it names the advert.
  2. Add a rule for that advert or its ad set.
  3. Use Reassign to place the lead that already arrived. Existing threads are never re-routed automatically, by design.
A branch with no manager notifies nobody

A rule can send leads to a showroom with no active store manager. The lead arrives correctly and then sits, because there is nobody to tell. Before adding a rule for a branch, make sure it has an active manager who can open Conversations.

Only the customer's words are read. The bot's own questions are excluded, or “What is your budget?” would score every conversation.

Edit it under Settings → Business Configuration → Lead scoring: phrases, weights, bands and the minimum number of messages.

Saving the policy freezes your own copy

The first time you save it, your organisation keeps its own copy and later improvements no longer reach you automatically. If the bot's wording changes, add the new phrases yourself.

A score is calculated when asked for and stored. Changing the policy does not rewrite old scores — press the refresh arrow on the Intent panel.

Campaigns and message templates

To message a customer outside the 24-hour window you need an approved template. Write one under Settings → Message Templates and submit it to Meta; approval usually takes minutes to a day. Approved templates become available in Campaigns, where you pick an audience and send. Consent is checked for every recipient, and anyone who opted out is excluded automatically.

Part Four

Selling

Quoting a piece, discounting it, taking the money and handling what comes back.

Quotations and orders

One quotation can carry several pieces — a necklace, earrings and a ring — each priced in full and totalled together.

Building one

  1. Open Quotation & Orders → New. Enter the customer's name and number.
  2. Choose the item type, and the style number if there is one. The download arrow pulls that design's materials in automatically.
  3. Fill the materials table — gold, stones, making.
  4. + Add item for a second piece. Each keeps its own metal, stones, making and discounts.
  5. Create quotation. GST is added and the total shown.

The materials table

Gold, each stone and the making charge are rows in one list, in the same order as the printed sheet. Every row has its own Dis %.

RowPriced asExample
Goldweight (g) × rate per gram, less its discount4.90 × 11,000 = 53,900
Diamondcarats × rate per carat, less its discount0.72 × 20,000 −5% = 13,680
Colour stonecarats × rate per carat, less its discount2.00 × 30,000 −5% = 57,000
Makinggold weight × making rate, less its discount4.90 × 1,200 −16.66% = 4,900

Those four lines total ₹1,29,480 before GST, matching the shop's own sheet exactly.

Finding a stone

Type what you know — round, VVS, ruby, or the code itself. The box searches shape, quality and group, not just the code. The carats box offers the standard per-stone weights.

Size, remark and metal colour

  • Size is set once for the piece — ring size 12, bangle 2.6, chain 16 — with cm, inch, or blank for Indian ring sizes.
  • Remark carries bespoke instructions: engraving, finish, a customer's own stone.
  • Metal colour is not asked. The metal code already says it: G14YG is 14K yellow, G14WG white.
If it will not save

The message names the row — “Item 1, stone 2 of 3: … needs its carats”. An empty row you added and did not use is ignored. A row with figures but no stone code must be completed or cleared.

The gold rate every new quote is priced from comes from Settings → Gold Rate. A quote freezes the rate it was priced at; if the rate is stale the system asks you to confirm today's before saving.

Discounts, payments and returns

Discounts

Beyond the per-row discounts in a quote, Discounts carries requests that need approval, with a live margin check as the figure is typed. Requests appear in Approvals for whoever may decide, and every decision is kept in the audit log.

Sales & Payments

Records a sale with its advance and balance, and tracks collection against it. Outstanding balances feed the follow-up lists, so a part-paid order does not quietly go cold.

Orders in production

A custom order from a quotation routes to production and appears under Orders in Production with its stage. Orders imported from the factory system appear here too, so one screen covers both.

Returns & Exchange

Returns, exchanges, buyback, repairs and old-gold trade-ins. Like discounts, a return above the permitted threshold raises an approval rather than completing silently.

Loyalty, referral and targets

  • Loyalty & Referral — gold-savings schemes and the referral wallet.
  • Points Programme — what a purchase earns and what a point is worth, including the key your own website uses.
  • Targets — monthly sales targets per store, with achievement tracked against them.
  • Sales Performance — the leaderboard, commission and incentives.
Part Five

People

Attendance, the daily sales report, and the roster behind them.

Attendance

Where attendance is recorded

Signing in does not mark anybody present. Everyone except head office is taken to the punch screen at /check-in, and the day is recorded only when a punch is made there.

SituationWhat happens
Inside the branch fence Checks in automatically the moment the phone confirms the location. The time is recorded by itself — nothing to tap.
Outside it Asks why, records the punch, and reports it.
GPS unclear, or no fix Same — a reason is required, then it is recorded.
Fence cannot be read Also asks for a reason. Not knowing where somebody is is never treated as knowing they are at work.

Check-out works the same way, from the punch screen or from HRMS.

Nobody is ever locked out

A punch is never refused for being in the wrong place. A phone inside a concrete showroom can be wrong by 50–150 metres, and refusing the punch does not move the person — it only removes the record that they came to work. The fence decides whether a punch is verified, not whether it is allowed.

A reason is still required. An unexplained punch from outside the branch would be indistinguishable from one person punching for another.

Head office is told

Every punch the fence cannot confirm notifies the branch manager and head office, carrying the distance, the allowed radius and the reason typed. The person who punched is not notified — they know where they were.

Putting it right — regularisation

  1. Open HRMS & Attendance. Flagged punches are marked.
  2. Read the reason and the distance.
  3. Accept it, or correct the record. Every change is kept in the audit log with who made it.

Branch fences

Each showroom has its own radius, set with the branch under Settings → Store Setup, where the current value for each branch is shown. Set it wide enough to absorb indoor drift in a concrete building — a phone indoors is routinely 50–150 m out — but short of the distance to the next showroom. Anything under about 100 m will flag staff who are standing at their own counter.

Roster and pay

Roster & Pay turns the attendance register into shifts, leave balances and payroll. Leave requests raised by staff appear in Approvals.

Daily reporting over WhatsApp

The day's sheet is filed by messaging the staff number. No app, no login.

Filing a report

  1. Message +91 72089 12616 and say hi.
  2. Choose 1 — send today's daily report.
  3. Answer each question. Reply 0 for none, or skip where offered.
  4. Check the summary, then reply YES to file, CANCEL to discard, or DATE 20/08 to file it for another day.

What it asks, in the order of the sheet

SectionQuestions
FootfallWalk-ins · serious enquiries · conversions
Table A — counter sale Sale value, then cash · card · UPI · old gold taken and its value
Table B — customised sale Booking value, open bookings, bookings closed, amount received, then its own cash · card · UPI · bank transfer · old gold
RemarkAnything worth noting about the day
It only asks what applies

Nothing sold at the counter? Those payment questions are skipped entirely. No old gold? It does not ask what the gold was worth. A quiet day is nine questions; a full trading day is twenty.

The two payment blocks are kept apart

Counter sale and customised sale each have their own cash, card and UPI, because the shop reconciles the two tables separately. A heading above each question says which table you are filling, and each block must add up to its own total or the bot asks again.

Where reports appear

Head office is notified as each report arrives. They are read under Reporting & DSR and can be downloaded as the standard DSR sheet in PDF or Excel. Reports filed by WhatsApp are marked as such, so they can be told apart from those entered on the dashboard. Scheduled Reports sends month-end summaries automatically, and Morning Digest tells each person what they owe a customer that day.

Checks worth making monthly

  • Every trading branch has an active store manager who can open Conversations.
  • New campaigns have a routing rule, or their leads land in the head-office queue.
  • The Meta access token is still valid — Settings → Integrations reports the connection state.
  • Branch coordinates and fence radii still match where the shops actually are.

Known limits

What the platform does not do today, so nobody plans around something that is not there.

The advert's WhatsApp greeting shows a broken character

Outstanding — needs correcting in Ads Manager

All three demo adverts store the greeting as Ã‰clat instead of Éclat. So somebody who taps the advert sees “Welcome to Ã‰clat Diamonds” in WhatsApp, with the message box pre-filled the same way. The platform's own replies are correct — the fault is in the advert text held at Meta, which is why it survived the fix made in the software.

Correcting it means a new advert creative, because this text cannot be edited in place. That produces a new post, so the advert links below change and the advert goes through review again.

A login ID cannot be changed

IDs are allocated when an account is created and are fixed. If somebody's ID is wrong, the only remedy is a new account — which starts their attendance history over.

Messaging is WhatsApp only

Instagram and Facebook Messenger are not connected as inboxes. An advert on either platform still arrives, because the conversation itself happens on WhatsApp.

Not yet built

  • Automatic reminders to staff who have not filed a daily report.
  • Uploading weekly and monthly PDFs for storage against a branch.
  • Outbound calling. The phone icon opens the device's own dialler; the system records calls that happened but does not place them.
