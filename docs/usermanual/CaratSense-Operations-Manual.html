1 · Getting in

Opening the address takes you straight to the sign-in screen. There is no public landing page to click past.

Two ways to sign in

MethodWho uses it
Login ID + password Head office, managers, and anyone an admin has set a password for.
WhatsApp OTP Shop-floor staff. They enter their mobile number and receive a code — no password to forget or share.

Login IDs follow a pattern

Accounts are created as firstname.storename@eclatdiamonds.in — for example anjali.mumbaibandra@eclatdiamonds.in. Staff are not told this automatically, so an admin must pass it on.

The most common sign-in failure

“Email ID not found” almost always means the person is guessing their login. Check the exact ID in Settings → Team and read it to them. Accounts created outside the pattern keep whatever address they were made with, and a login ID cannot be changed afterwards.

Where each role lands

RoleSeesLands on
Head officeEvery branch, every screen, all reporting.Dashboards
Store managerTheir own branch: inbox, leads, team, attendance.Punch screen
SalespersonTheir own branch's customers and their own attendance.Punch screen

Everyone except head office lands on the attendance punch screen, which passes them through to their own home once the day is started.

2 · Giving people access

Add a person — Settings → Team

  1. Open Settings → Team and choose Add staff.
  2. Enter their name, mobile number and branch, and pick a role.
  3. Optionally set a password now. If you leave it blank they sign in by WhatsApp OTP instead.
  4. The screen shows their Login ID. Write it down and give it to them — nothing sends it automatically.

Set or reset a password — Settings → Stores

Not on the Team screen

Password reset lives under Settings → Stores: open the branch, find the person, choose Reset password. The Team screen tells you to “reset on their row”, which is misleading — the control is on Stores.

Choose which screens a person can open — Settings → Access

Head office can grant or withdraw individual screens per person. Leave it alone and each role gets its standard set, which is what most people need.

One setting that silently breaks lead routing

A store manager who cannot open Conversations is skipped when the system looks for someone to own a new lead. The lead reaches the branch and sits unowned. If leads stop being assigned, check this first.

Grant daily-report access — Settings → WhatsApp Reporting

  1. Open Settings → WhatsApp Reporting. Everyone in your branches is listed, with who can already file.
  2. Find the person and choose Set up. A short code appears and is copied to your clipboard. It is valid for 10 minutes.
  3. Read the code to them. They send it from the handset they want to use, to the staff reporting number.
  4. That handset is now bound to them. Remove on their row undoes it.
Why you never type their phone number

The number is learnt from the message carrying the code, so nobody can bind a handset they are not holding. The bot also cannot message a number that has never written to it — WhatsApp only allows a business to open a conversation with an approved template, and a one-time code is not something Meta approves.

Two separate numbers, two separate jobs. A message is answered by whichever number it arrives on.

What the customer bot asks

  1. What are you looking for today? (engagement ring, wedding jewellery, daily wear, other)
  2. Approximate budget (under ₹50K · ₹50K–₹1L · ₹1L–₹2L · above ₹2L)
  3. When do you need it (within 7 days · this month · in a few months · just exploring)
  4. Shall we call you (yes now · later · not now)

Customers may answer in their own words — the options are a prompt, not a menu. After the last answer the bot stops and the conversation is handed to the branch, which is when the manager is notified.

One phone, both bots, no collision

The same mobile number can hold a customer conversation on one line and a staff reporting conversation on the other at the same time. Neither overwrites the other. A staff member messaging the customer number is answered as a customer, so they can test it safely.

The bot stops once a person takes over

After hand-over a thread is marked for a human and the bot stays silent in it. This is deliberate. When testing, always use a number that has never written in before, or it will look as though nothing is happening.

4 · Conversations & the CRM

What happens when a lead arrives

  1. The customer taps an advert or writes to the customer number.
  2. If they came from an advert, the advert is matched to a showroom by a routing rule and the conversation is stamped with that branch.
  3. The bot asks its four questions.
  4. The conversation is handed to the branch's store manager, who gets a notification with the customer's name.
  5. The thread shows the advert they tapped, their answers, and a lead score.

Moving a lead to someone else

Open the conversation and use Reassign. Choose the branch first, then the owner — the list only offers people who actually work at that branch. The new owner is notified; the person who made the change is not.

The 24-hour rule

Why the message box sometimes will not type

WhatsApp allows a free reply only within 24 hours of the customer's last message. After that the box is disabled and the screen says so. An approved template can still reach them — use Send a template.

This is WhatsApp's rule, not a setting. The clock restarts every time the customer writes again.

Reading the inbox

What you seeWhat it means
Facebook Ad / Instagram Ad Where the lead came from, read from the advert itself.
Needs a person The bot has finished and somebody must reply.
Routing check A later advert disagreed with where this thread already sits. The thread was left alone for a person to decide.
No store yet No routing rule matched, so no branch owns it. It waits in the head office queue.
Last wrote … When the customer last messaged — this drives the 24-hour rule.

5 · Advert routing

A rule sends each campaign's leads to the showroom that paid for it, so the right manager is notified within seconds.

Setting a rule — Settings → Business configuration → Ad routing

Match onUse when
Ad-set name contains The recommended choice. One rule per showroom, matching the words your marketing team already puts in the ad-set name — “Bandra”, “Udaipur”. Every future advert in that ad set routes with no further work.
Exact ad ID Pinning one specific advert somewhere different, or a campaign whose ad-set name carries no location.

Where two rules both match, the more specific wins — “Bandra Broadway” beats “Bandra” without any manual ordering.

Ad-set name matching needs the Meta Ads connection

A Click-to-WhatsApp tap tells us the advert's ID and nothing else. To turn that into an ad-set name, connect Meta Ads under Settings → Integrations with an access token that can read adverts. Without it, only exact-ad-ID rules can match.

If a lead arrives unrouted

  1. Open the thread and read the advert card — it names the advert.
  2. Add a rule for that advert or its ad set.
  3. Use Reassign to place the lead that already arrived. Existing threads are never re-routed automatically, by design.
A branch with no manager notifies nobody

A rule can send leads to a showroom that has no active store manager. The lead arrives correctly and then sits, because there is nobody to tell. Before adding a rule for a branch, make sure it has an active manager who can open Conversations.

6 · Lead scoring

Every conversation is scored out of 100 so a manager can see at a glance who is worth calling first.

Where the number comes from

The score is built from phrases your own policy lists, each carrying a weight. It is arithmetic, not a guess, and the panel shows exactly which signals fired.

SignalWeightRecognises
Says they want to buy+30“want to buy”, “ready to buy”, “yes, call me now”
Mentions a budget+20“budget”, “under ₹50k”, “above ₹2L”
Needs it soon+20“this week”, “within 7 days”, “urgent”
Wants to visit+15“visit”, “appointment”, “timings”
Asks about a specific item+10“do you have”, “in stock”
Comparing with others+5“cheaper”, “other shop”
Raising a complaint−25“refund”, “broken”, “not happy”
Not interested−40“stop messaging”, “remove my number”

Only the customer's words are read. The bot's own questions are excluded, or “What is your budget?” would score every conversation.

Editing the policy

Settings → Business configuration → Lead scoring. Phrases, weights, bands and the minimum number of messages are all yours to change.

Changing the policy freezes your own copy

The first time you save the policy, your organisation keeps its own copy. Improvements shipped later no longer reach you automatically — if the bot's wording changes, add the new phrases yourself.

Re-scoring

A score is calculated when it is asked for and stored. Changing the policy does not rewrite old scores — press the refresh arrow on the Intent panel to score a conversation again under the current rules.

7 · Quotations

One quotation can carry several pieces — a necklace, earrings and a ring — each priced in full and totalled together.

Building one

  1. Open Quotations → New. Enter the customer's name and number.
  2. Choose the item type, and the style number if there is one. The download arrow pulls that design's materials in automatically.
  3. Fill the materials table — gold, stones, making.
  4. + Add item for a second piece. Each keeps its own metal, stones, making and discounts.
  5. Create quotation. GST is added and the total shown.

The materials table

Gold, each stone and the making charge are rows in one list, in the same order as the printed sheet. Every row has its own Dis %.

RowPriced asExample
Goldweight (g) × rate per gram, less its discount 4.90 × 11,000 = 53,900
Diamondcarats × rate per carat, less its discount 0.72 × 20,000 −5% = 13,680
Colour stonecarats × rate per carat, less its discount 2.00 × 30,000 −5% = 57,000
Makinggold weight × making rate, less its discount 4.90 × 1,200 −16.66% = 4,900

Those four lines total ₹1,29,480 before GST, matching the shop's own sheet exactly.

Finding a stone

Type what you know — round, VVS, ruby, or the code itself. The box searches shape, quality and group, not just the code. The carats box offers the standard per-stone weights.

Size, remark and the rest

  • Size is set once for the piece — ring size 12, bangle 2.6, chain 16 — with cm, inch or blank for Indian ring sizes.
  • Remark carries bespoke instructions: engraving, finish, a customer's own stone.
  • Metal colour is not asked. The metal code already says it: G14YG is 14K yellow, G14WG white.
If it will not save

The message names the row — “Item 1, stone 2 of 3: … needs its carats”. An empty row you added and did not use is ignored, so you can leave it. A row with figures but no stone code must be completed or cleared.

8 · Attendance

Where attendance is recorded

Signing in does not mark anybody present. Everyone except head office is taken to the punch screen at /check-in, and the day is only recorded when a punch is made there.

SituationWhat happens
Inside the branch fence Checks in automatically the moment the phone confirms the location. Nothing to tap.
Outside it, or GPS unclear Asks for a reason, records the punch, and flags it for review.
No location at all Same — a reason is required, then it is recorded.

Check-out works the same way, from the punch screen or from HRMS.

Nobody is ever locked out

A punch is never refused for being in the wrong place. A phone inside a concrete showroom can be wrong by 50–150 metres, and refusing the punch does not move the person — it only removes the record that they came to work. The fence decides whether a punch is verified, not whether it is allowed.

A reason is still required. An unexplained punch from outside the branch would be indistinguishable from one person punching for another.

Head office is told

Every punch the fence cannot confirm raises a notification to the branch manager and head office, carrying the distance, the allowed radius and the reason that was typed. The person who punched is not notified — they know where they were.

Putting it right — regularisation

  1. Open HRMS → Attendance. Flagged punches are marked.
  2. Read the reason and the distance.
  3. Accept it, or correct the record. Every change is kept in the audit log with who made it.

Branch fences

Each showroom has its own radius, set with the branch under Settings → Stores. Mumbai Bandra is set to 250 m — wide enough to absorb indoor drift in a concrete building, far short of the next showroom.

9 · Daily reporting over WhatsApp

The day's sheet is filed by messaging the staff number. No app, no login.

Filing a report

  1. Message +91 72089 12616 and say hi.
  2. Choose 1 — send today's daily report.
  3. Answer each question. Reply 0 for none, or skip where offered.
  4. Check the summary, then reply YES to file it, CANCEL to discard, or DATE 20/08 to file it for another day.

What it asks, in the order of the sheet

SectionQuestions
Footfall Walk-ins · serious enquiries · conversions
Table A — counter sale Sale value, then cash · card · UPI · old gold taken and its value
Table B — customised sale Booking value, open bookings, bookings closed, amount received, then its own cash · card · UPI · bank transfer · old gold
Remark Anything worth noting about the day
It only asks what applies

Nothing sold at the counter? The counter payment questions are skipped entirely. No old gold? It does not ask what the gold was worth. A quiet day is nine questions; a full trading day is twenty.

The two payment blocks are kept apart

Counter sale and customised sale each have their own cash, card and UPI — because the shop reconciles the two tables separately. The heading above each question says which table you are filling. Each block must also add up to its own total, or the bot asks again.

Where reports appear

Head office is notified as each report arrives. They are read under Reporting → Daily, and can be downloaded as the standard DSR sheet in PDF or Excel. Reports filed by WhatsApp are marked as such, so they can be told apart from those entered on the dashboard.

10 · Known limits

What the system does not do today, so nobody plans around something that is not there.

Adverts cannot be shared as a link

There is no clickable advert URL

Click-to-WhatsApp adverts are built as unpublished posts. They have no public web address, and the preview link from Ads Manager only opens for somebody signed in to the ad account. To show the advert journey to somebody, either let Meta deliver the advert to them, or tap it yourself while sharing your screen.

A login ID cannot be changed

IDs are allocated when an account is created and are fixed. If somebody's ID is wrong, the only remedy is a new account — which starts their attendance history over.

Messaging is WhatsApp only

Instagram and Facebook Messenger are not connected as inboxes. An advert on either platform still arrives, because the conversation itself happens on WhatsApp.

Not yet built

  • Automatic reminders to staff who have not filed a daily report.
  • Uploading weekly and monthly PDFs for storage against a branch.
  • Outbound calling. The phone icon opens the device's own dialler; the system records calls that happened but does not place them.

Things worth checking monthly

  • Every trading branch has an active store manager who can open Conversations.
  • New campaigns have a routing rule, or their leads land in the head office queue.
  • The Meta access token is still valid — Settings → Integrations reports the connection state.
